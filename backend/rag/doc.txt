# WhatShoppy Navigation Assistant Knowledge Base

The WhatShoppy Navigation Assistant helps the business owner understand how to navigate and use the WhatShoppy mobile application.

The assistant only answers questions about the application interfaces, navigation, and usage.
It should not answer general questions outside WhatShoppy.

The assistant should answer in English by default.
If the user asks in French, the assistant can answer in French.
If the user asks in Arabic, the assistant can answer simply in English unless Arabic support is added.

The assistant should give short, clear, and helpful answers.
When the user asks how to do something, the assistant should answer with simple steps.
The assistant should not expose technical API errors, backend errors, or internal system details to the user.

---

# Home and Dashboard Interface

The Home or Dashboard interface is the main screen shown after the business owner signs in.

The Dashboard gives the business owner a quick overview of the business activity.

The Dashboard can show:
- total revenue
- pending orders
- total clients
- total orders
- total products
- quick actions

The business owner can use the Dashboard to quickly understand the current state of the store.

If the business owner wants to see business statistics, they should open the Dashboard or Home interface.

If the business owner asks where to see revenue, the answer is: open the Dashboard or Home interface.

If the business owner asks where to see pending orders, the answer is: open the Dashboard or Home interface, or go to the Orders interface for more details.

Example questions:
- What does the dashboard show?
- Where can I see my revenue?
- Where can I see pending orders?
- Where can I see business statistics?

Expected answer:
Open the Dashboard or Home interface. It shows an overview of your business, including revenue, pending orders, clients, orders, products, and quick actions.

Example questions:

- Where can I see orders?

- How can I check pending orders?

Expected answer:
Go to the Orders interface. Open the order you want to manage, select the new status, and save the change. The available statuses are Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, and Cancelled.

# Add Product Interface

The Add Product interface allows the business owner to create a new product.

The Add Product form can contain:
- product name
- SKU
- category
- description
- price
- stock quantity
- product image

To add a product manually:
1. Open the Stock or Products interface.
2. Tap Add Product.
3. Fill in the product name.
4. Enter the SKU if required.
5. Select or create a category.
6. Add the product description.
7. Enter the product price.
8. Enter the stock quantity.
9. Add a product image if needed.
10. Tap Add Product to save the product.

The product is saved in the Supabase database after the business owner taps Add Product.

If the business owner wants to create a new category, they can use the plus button next to the Category field.

Example questions:
- How can I add a product?
- Where is the Add Product button?
- What fields do I need to add a product?
- How can I create a category?
- How can I save a new product?

Expected answer:
Go to the Stock or Products interface, then tap Add Product. Fill in the product name, SKU, category, description, price, stock, and image. Finally, tap Add Product to save it.

# AI Image Analysis in Add Product

The AI image analysis feature helps the business owner create a product faster using a product image.

To use AI image analysis:
1. Open the Stock or Products interface.
2. Tap Add Product.
3. Upload or select a product image.
4. Tap Generate Automatically or start the AI analysis.
5. The AI analyzes the image.
6. The system suggests product information.
7. Review the generated fields.
8. Edit the information if needed.
9. Enter the missing fields manually.
10. Tap Add Product to save the product.

The AI can generate or suggest:
- product category
- product name or title
- product description
- estimated price if this feature is available

The business owner should review the AI result before saving the product.

The business owner may still need to enter:
- price
- stock quantity
- SKU if required

The AI image analysis does not save the product automatically.
The product is saved only after the business owner taps Add Product.

Example questions:
- How does AI analysis work?
- How can I generate product information automatically?
- How can I analyze a product image?
- What does Generate Automatically do?
- Does the AI save the product automatically?
- What fields does the AI generate?

Expected answer:
Go to Add Product, upload a product image, then tap Generate Automatically. The AI analyzes the image and suggests the product category, name, description, and possibly an estimated price. Review the result, complete missing fields like price and stock, then tap Add Product to save.

# Authentication and Sign In

The business owner accesses the application through the Sign In screen.

To sign in:
1. Open the application.
2. Enter the email address.
3. Enter the password.
4. Tap Sign In.

After signing in, the business owner is redirected to the Home or Dashboard interface.

If the business owner forgets the password, they can use the Forgot Password option if it is available.

Example questions:
- How can I sign in?
- What happens after login?
- Where do I go after signing in?
- How can I recover my password?

Expected answer:
Enter your email and password on the Sign In screen, then tap Sign In. After login, you will be redirected to the Dashboard.
